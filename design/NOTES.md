## Symbols

- M_wb: wet basis moisture, water divided by wet grain, kg/kg
- omega: humidity ratio, vapour per dry air, kg/kg
- phi: relative humidity as a fraction
- rho_b: bulk density of paddy, taken as 560 kg/m3
- epsilon: bed voidage, taken as 0.45
- beta: open area ratio of the holes

## Materials

- Inner shell: stainless steel 304, grain contact and moist air
- Outer absorber: galvanised steel, matte black outside only
- Plenum and clean air duct: galvanised steel
- Biomass chamber and flue: mild steel with a lining, flue isolated
- Stand: mild steel angle, clear of the hot shell

## Holes

- Inner: 1.5 mm on a 6 mm triangular pitch, about 5.7 percent open
- Outer vents: 2 mm on 12 mm pitch, about 2.5 percent open, upper half only
- Check against paddy thickness, about 1.7 to 2.2 mm
- Brokens may still pass. A food contact mesh is the fallback.

## Safety

- Flue gas stays out of the grain
- Independent over temperature cut out
- Grain temperature at three heights
- Stop or reduce heat if grain approaches 50 C
- First trial air: 40 to 45 C
